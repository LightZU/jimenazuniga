Jimena Zúñiga
After graduating in Spatial Design from the École Boulle and training at the Massana School of Design in Barcelona, I chose to work with light as my main medium. A professional degree in Lighting Design and Management in Lyon allowed me to deepen my technical knowledge of this tool, which I think through and apply in my projects. After working in agencies, I now develop my own projects, combining design and light through an independent practice. Today, my work finds its balance between a collaborative approach, with various lighting design studios, and a personal reflection that I pursue in my projects.
