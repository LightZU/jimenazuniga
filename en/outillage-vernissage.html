Outillage Vernissage
Outillage Vernissage by Céline Pelcé as part of Paris Design Week 2018. A lighting design favouring warm tones to accompany and welcome the guests in this culinary experience. The food, its colours, its textures and the materials that go with it create a play of light, reflections and shine that stimulates our senses during the tasting.
