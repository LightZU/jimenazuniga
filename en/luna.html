Luna
Luna is a wall light in Sidobre granite. Its bush-hammered texture, lit by grazing light, reveals a lunar surface with multiple glints. In collaboration with Victor Mauro. Made with the Ateliers de Vabre Granit, the Granit et Pierres du Sidobre association, SVTU and iGuzzini.

«La Madre Luna, hoy vestida de toda su redondez, los bañaba con su mejor luz…» Rafael Dumett, El espía del Inca.
