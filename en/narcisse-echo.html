Narcisse & Écho

Narcisse & Écho is a project inspired by the Greek myth. We imagined a space of contemplation where sound and light are the protagonists. A place of contemplation with shimmering reflections which, appealing to our senses, leads us towards new perceptions. The spirit of these two figures is brought back into the room through the furniture: a mirrored portico and a seat with special acoustics. In collaboration with Victor Mauro.
