Enso Variations

ENSŌ, “circle” in Japanese, is the calligraphic gesture drawn in a single stroke, in which body and mind are set free. Céline Thibault and Géraud Pellottiero turned it into a monumental sculptural apparatus: two brackets of bent steel and a rotating ring, suspended in mid-air.

Lighting design for Nuit Blanche 2023, at the Halle des Blancs-Manteaux. The light follows the curves of the steel and extends the acrobats’ movements through their projected shadows. A rosy hue answers the blue floor, in the spirit of a utopian gym. This contrast also makes the textured light drawn on the floor vibrate.

With the support of iGuzzini and Soka Lighting.
