Pédalo Dynamo

Pédalo Dynamo is a piece of festival furniture born from a summery imagination. An installation proposed by Victor Mauro for the We Love Green festival in 2017. A lantern to light your way: with a little effort, dynamo technology lets you recharge your phone battery and also light up a lantern to see and to signal, creating a meeting point for festival-goers. A light with cool shimmers, recalling moonlight during these musical nights.
